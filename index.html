About

We build websites the way we'd want ours built: clear, fast and easy to own.

Aina is a web studio that helps businesses show up properly online. Whether you need a brand-new site, a redesign of one that's gone stale, or SEO so people can actually find you, we handle it end to end.

We keep teams small and conversations direct. You talk to the people building your site, and what we hand over is something you can understand, update and grow.

  2. 02

    Design & build

    You see designs and working pages early, and shape them as we go.

  3. 03

    Launch & support

    We take it live, show you how it works and stay around afterwards.
