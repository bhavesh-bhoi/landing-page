Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aina Tech Services LLP ("Aina", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects your personal information when you visit our website (ainatech.in), contact us or use our Services. We respect your privacy and handle your information in line with applicable Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, the service you are interested in and the message you write when you use our contact form, email us or call us.
  • Appointment details: if you book an appointment, the details you enter in the booking form (for example name, contact details, your website address and preferred time).
  • Technical information: basic data your browser sends to any website, such as IP address, browser type and the pages you open.

2. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiries and give you quotations.
  • To schedule and run appointments about website redesign.
  • To deliver and support the Services you ask for.
  • To keep our website secure and working properly.
  • To meet legal and tax obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Appointment booking form

Our appointment form may be provided by a third-party form or scheduling service and shown inside our website. The information you enter there is processed by that provider under its own privacy policy, as well as being received by us.

4. Cookies and third-party resources

Our website does not use advertising cookies. To display the page, your browser loads fonts and scripts from third-party providers (such as Google Fonts, cdnjs and jsDelivr), and those providers may receive your IP address and browser details. If we add analytics or similar tools in future, we will update this policy.

5. Sharing your information

We share personal information only when needed to provide our Services (for example with hosting, email or scheduling providers who work for us), when you ask us to, or when the law requires it. We ask the providers we use to protect your information.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information only for as long as needed for the purposes above, including to follow up on enquiries, deliver projects and meet legal or accounting requirements. After that we delete it or anonymise it.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

You may ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct or update it;
  • erase it, where we are not required to keep it; and
  • withdraw your consent to our using it.

To make a request or raise a concern, write to us at info@ainatech.in. We will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Children

Our Services are meant for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Links to other websites

Our website may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version.

12. Contact us
